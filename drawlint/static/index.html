Patent drawing sheet check

Check a drawing sheet (SVG) or a set of sheets (PDF, one sheet per page) against the drawing formality rules of 37 CFR 1.84: sheet size, margins, black ink, line weight, text height, sheet numbers and FIG. labels. Free while in test.

Automated formality check only, not legal advice.

or drop the file here

Up to 20 MB and 100 pages. The file is checked in memory and not stored.

Errors are formality problems under the rule cited. Advisories are worth a look but may be fine. Passing does not guarantee acceptance, and the check may miss problems or flag ones that are not there. PDF pages are read as drawn vector content; a scanned PDF is one image per page and its content cannot be checked.

Rule text: 37 CFR 1.84.
